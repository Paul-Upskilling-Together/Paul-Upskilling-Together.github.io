Where people get stuck

Two out of three who start a booking finish it. The ones who don't are dropping out at the form — and more so on a phone than a computer. That's a fixable thing, and now you can see it.

What subjects parents want

Including the ones you don't teach yet. In this sample, geometry and test prep came up most often. That's your hiring list, written by your customers.

What's next

What this first version doesn't do yet.

You mentioned that keeping schedule changes straight is the messy part. We've made a start, but here's what's honestly still on your plate.

  • ReschedulingA parent can cancel, but not move a session to a different time. That's the next thing we'd build.
  • Telling the tutorYou'll be emailed on every booking. Automatically passing it to the tutor comes next.
  • Your Google SheetThe site doesn't replace it yet. We'd want to watch a few real weeks before touching how your schedule is stored.
  • Watching a real visitPostHog can replay an actual parent's session, so you can see exactly where someone got stuck rather than guessing.
